Overview

Breezy is a personal weather companion — clean, private, and currently in beta. Because the app is deliberately simple, the experience of checking the weather stays calm and focused, without the clutter of a traditional weather app.

What It Does

  • View local weather beautifully and privately.
  • Privacy-first — no unnecessary tracking or noise.
  • A clean, minimal iOS interface built with SwiftUI.
